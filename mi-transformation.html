MI transformation

Reporting that covers the whole HR lifecycle.

I design and build predictive, high-impact MI that unearths hidden bottlenecks, operational strains, and flight risks - turning fragmented legacy data into the exact insights the business needs to operate and course-correct.

The HR Intelligence engine shows the breadth I cover, from hire to exit. Where you need something deeper, such as a model for year-end reward, recruitment tracking and source analysis, employee relations tracking and reporting, or data for a data room, I build it to what you need.

Across the lifecycle

Thirteen stages, and what I report on in each.

Across all of it: I work directly with HR and Finance teams, reporting independently across the whole HR lifecycle, partnered with the relevant HR managers.

01Attract and recruit

Sourcing activity tracked by department and recruiter. Dual-lens dashboards that measure the business impact of a hire, alongside real-time KPIs holding recruitment managers accountable for funnel bottlenecks and time-to-hire.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Recruitment Open roles, the funnel from application to hire, source of hire, time to fill and time to accept, and recruiters side by side.
  • Cross-Domain Explorer Any recruiting figure cut by any people attribute, for the question nobody planned a report for.
02Onboard

Onboarding Intelligence: Tracks overdue probationary reviews, identifies new-starter risk bands, and pinpoints where recent hires are struggling. Early Attrition: Tracks leavers inside their first year. Survey Desk: Automates bespoke new-hire satisfaction surveys, generating send lists tailored to your actual onboarding rhythm.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Onboarding Intelligence Day 30, 60 and 90 checkpoints, who is overdue, new-starter risk bands, and where new starters are struggling by team or location.
  • Attrition & Leavers Leavers inside their first year, by manager and team.
  • Survey Desk Who is due a ninety-day new-starter survey, with the send list and the questionnaire.
03Learn and develop

Compliance: Tracks mandatory regulatory training expiries. Development ROI & Educator KPIs: Joins learning data against performance to reveal if development retains talent, while simultaneously tracking the performance and impact of your internal educators.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Learning & Compliance Mandatory training compliance by team, overdue items, and the learning delivered in the month.
  • Top Talent Who ranks highest and what they have in common, to steer development.
04Perform and calibrate

Year-end performance calibration and distribution curve tracking. Maps performance scores against pay and flight risk, ensuring top performers are identified and protected.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Performance The rating distribution, and where ratings cluster by team, so calibration can check whether the pattern reflects performance or how ratings were given.
  • 9-Boxer: talent review and calibration Every person rated for what they achieved and how they went about it; the grid, the proposed pay and bonus, and workbooks to send to business owners and collect back.
  • Team Signals Teams where several warning signs appear at once, as a prompt to find out why.
  • Employee Scorecard One person's picture across absence, pay, performance and training.
05Reward and recognise

Compa-ratio analysis, pay equity tracking, and bonus distribution modelling. Reconciles reward data against performance ratings to ensure compensation is genuinely merit-based and equitable.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Reward A salary review modelled against budget, the people it would take past their band, and proposals flagged for review.
  • Pay Equity The gender pay gap, quartiles, and pay gaps within a role.
  • 9-Boxer: pay and bonus pots Pay-rise and bonus pots by division, and what the ratings ask for against each.
  • Statutory Returns The official returns that draw on pay and workforce data.
06Engage and listen

Joins engagement sentiment directly against attrition, absence, and performance. Exposes where high engagement masks burnout, or where low engagement serves as an early warning for turnover.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Engagement Response, eNPS and wellbeing signals, and how people rate their line management.
  • Culture Signals Teams where culture signals point the same way.
  • Survey Desk Who is due a new-starter survey at ninety days or an exit survey on leaving, the send list and the questionnaire, without sending data anywhere.
07Diversity and inclusion

Demographic representation, gender and ethnicity pay gap precursors, and progression tracking. Identifies structural bottlenecks where diverse talent enters the organisation but fails to advance.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Culture & Diversity Gender and ethnicity mix, seniority, and women in leadership by division.
  • Pay Equity Pay gaps by gender, and ethnicity and disability where declared.
  • Statutory Returns Equality and gender pay returns.
08Attendance and wellbeing

Absence Management: Open absence reporting to flag unclosed records and monitor Bradford Factor triggers. Holiday & Burnout: Audits mandatory two-week continuous block leave for regulated roles, preventing year-end operational bottlenecks and unearthing burnout risk.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Absence The absence rate, by type, department and manager, and where it is moving.
  • Absence Pulse The current month against the pattern.
  • Absence Trends Seasonal and weekday patterns, return-to-work follow-up and spreading signals.
  • Absence Watch Teams and individuals who have crossed a trigger.
  • Open Absences Who is off today.
  • Long-Term Absence Long spells and how long they have run.
  • Leave Balance Holiday not yet taken and the year-end rush it creates.
  • Holiday History by Group Holiday requests, approvals and declines by group.
09Employee relations and conduct

Caseload volumes, grievance timelines, and time-to-resolve metrics. Spots manager hot-spots where employee relations issues repeatedly surface, allowing HR to intervene before formal action is needed.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Employee Relations Open cases ranked by risk, ACAS timelines, and whether similar cases were treated alike across managers.
  • Team Signals Teams with several cases or signals at once.
10Talent and succession

Bench strength and critical role cover. Identifies single points of failure where a key manager has no credible deputy within one grade step, mapping structural risk directly to the P&L.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Critical Roles The roles the organisation cannot afford to lose and how well covered they are.
  • Succession Search Who could step up, searched across the population.
  • Top Talent Ranked talent view for strategic review.
  • 9-Boxer: talent review The grid of what and how across the organisation, with the unbalanced boxes marked for discussion.
  • Org View Any manager's team with its measures.
  • Org Chart A printable organisation chart.
11Retain

Predictive flight risk. Joins absence, pay position, performance, and engagement to surface teams statistically drifting away from the organisation, enabling intervention before they resign.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Retention Risk Everyone ranked by flight risk, with the factors behind each score.
  • Watch Register The patterns that are forming now, and who they involve.
  • Attrition & Leavers Attrition by manager, tenure and reason, and regrettable leavers.
12Exit

Regretted loss tracking and exit interview sentiment analysis. Maps why people are leaving against their managers, testing historical data to see the real patterns preceding a resignation.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • Survey Desk Who is due an exit survey, whenever they leave, with the send list and the questionnaire.
  • Exit Surveys Why people leave, which departures looked preventable, and what leavers said about their line management.
  • Leaver Management Who is leaving and the backfill position.
  • Future-Dated Leavers Everyone with a leaving date still to come.
  • Attrition & Leavers The leaver rate and profile.
13Core HR, structure and finance

One Version of the Truth: Headcount, FTE, and establishment gaps agreed between HR and Finance. Resolves data conflicts and provides a rigorous FTE governance model the resource committee will accept.

In the HR Intelligence engine

  • FTE & Governance Headcount, FTE, contract mix, establishment and movement.
  • Org Structure Layers, spans of control and single points of failure.
  • Reconciliation Which records join the staff list and which do not.
  • Data Health What is wrong in the files, ranked by effect on the numbers.
  • Mapping Desk The few columns that need a decision, one click each.
  • Migration Readiness Whether the data is ready to move to a new system.

What I do

MI the board can act on, and numbers that agree.

  1. 01

    Cross-Lifecycle Predictive Analytics

    When your reports never talk to each other. Recruitment, performance, employee relations, and leavers read together to answer what single reports cannot. Uncover whether healthy averages are masking critical failure points.

  2. 02

    One Version of the Truth: HR and Finance

    When HR and Finance present conflicting headcount figures, executive confidence drops immediately. The board meeting shifts from strategic planning to questioning data provenance. I reconcile headcount, FTE, and payroll between HR and Finance, identifying and explaining every underlying variance. I build robust FTE governance models for resource committees so the executive team receives one trusted baseline.

  3. 03

    Regulatory & Compliance MI

    When you owe the board or regulator a number. Diversity MI, ER reporting, and regulatory returns built as Power BI projects your own team can own, with a flawless audit trail back to the source.

  4. 04

    M&A Data Rooms & Due Diligence

    When an organisation is being sold or merged, buyers will use messy workforce data to challenge operational stability and delay due diligence. I prepare the HR side of data rooms for sales and divestments, rigorously auditing and reconciling headcount and liability data so it stands up to hostile scrutiny under extreme time pressure.

  5. 05

    MI Architecture Design

    When reporting describes the past but needs to explain it. An agreed catalogue of measures mapped to the questions the business actually asks, delivered as a prioritised build plan.

  6. 06

    A monthly insight service

    Your exports each month, cleaned, reconciled and read, with a short written read-out of what moved, why, and what to watch next month. Delivered as a pack your board can take as it stands.

  7. 07

    HR and people data management

    Owning the truth of your data: stringent data governance, management information, and reconciling HR headcount directly with Finance so the executive board and resource committees never see a conflicting number.

  8. 08

    Complex HR projects

    Strategic execution under extreme pressure: highly scrutinised data rooms for corporate sales and divestments, robust FTE governance models, and seamless year-end performance calibration and reward modelling.
