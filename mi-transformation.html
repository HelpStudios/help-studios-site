MI transformation

Reporting that covers the whole HR lifecycle.

The HR Intelligence engine shows the breadth I can cover, from hire to exit. You do not have to use it. If you need a deeper model for year-end reward, recruitment tracking and source analysis, or employee relations tracking and reporting, or you need data for a data room, I build to what you need.

What I do

  1. 01

    Cross-Lifecycle Predictive Analytics

    When your reports never talk to each other. Recruitment, performance, employee relations, and leavers read together to answer what single reports cannot. Uncover whether healthy averages are masking critical failure points.

  2. 02

    One Version of the Truth: HR and Finance

    When HR and Finance present conflicting headcount figures, executive confidence drops immediately. The board meeting shifts from strategic planning to questioning data provenance. I reconcile headcount, FTE, and payroll between HR and Finance, identifying and explaining every underlying variance. I build robust FTE governance models for resource committees so the executive team receives one trusted baseline.

  3. 03

    Regulatory & Compliance MI

    When you owe the board or regulator a number. Diversity MI, ER reporting, and regulatory returns built as Power BI projects your own team can own, with a flawless audit trail back to the source.

  4. 04

    M&A Data Rooms & Due Diligence

    When an organisation is being sold or merged, buyers will use messy workforce data to challenge operational stability and delay due diligence. I prepare the HR side of data rooms for sales and divestments, rigorously auditing and reconciling headcount and liability data so it stands up to hostile scrutiny under extreme time pressure.

  5. 05

    MI Architecture Design

    When reporting describes the past but needs to explain it. An agreed catalogue of measures mapped to the questions the business actually asks, delivered as a prioritised build plan.

  6. 06

    A monthly insight service

    Your exports each month, cleaned, reconciled and read, with a short written read-out of what moved, why, and what to watch next month. Delivered as a pack your board can take as it stands.
