Power BI compatibility

The engine calculates. Power BI renders. The numbers always agree.

Power BI is not a second set of numbers to keep in step. The HR Intelligence engine is the single calculation authority, and it builds the Power BI dataset from exactly the figures it shows on screen, so a report in front of the board carries the same numbers as the screen it came from.

One calculation authority

A report is only as trustworthy as the calculation behind it. Here the calculation happens once, in the engine, and Power BI is the rendering surface. The dataset is generated from the same engine output that fills the on-screen reports, so the two cannot drift apart by being computed twice.

What the export gives your team

  • A Power BI-ready dataset: dimension tables for people, organisation and period, and fact tables for the measures, with a metric dimension that carries each metric’s definition.
  • Measures that match the engine: the DAX in the supplied template reads the engine’s figures, it does not recalculate them independently.
  • Your own pages on top: analysts can extend the report with their own visuals and slicers without changing the underlying numbers.

It stays yours

The export is a file. Nothing is uploaded from this site. Your team opens it in Power BI Desktop or publishes it to your own workspace, on your own tenancy, under your own controls.
