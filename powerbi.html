Power BI compatibility

The engine calculates. Power BI renders. The numbers always agree.

One set of numbers, not two. The engine is the single calculation authority; it builds the Power BI dataset from the same figures shown on screen, so the board report cannot drift from the engine.

One calculation authority

A report is only as trustworthy as the calculation behind it. Here the calculation happens once, in the engine; Power BI is the surface it is drawn on. The dataset is generated from that same output, so the two cannot disagree by being computed twice.

What the export gives your team

  • A Power BI-ready dataset: dimension tables for people, organisation and period, and fact tables for the measures, with a metric dimension that carries each metric’s definition.
  • Measures that match the engine: the DAX in the supplied template reads the engine’s figures, it does not recalculate them independently.
  • Your own pages on top: analysts can extend the report with their own visuals and slicers without changing the underlying numbers.

It stays yours

The export is a file. Nothing leaves this site. Your team opens it in Power BI Desktop, or publishes it to your own workspace, on your own controls.

Rendered in the site theme; figures are synthetic Meridian Bank demo data. Real Power BI captures are being re-taken in the report theme.
