HRIS migration & reporting

Move to a new HRIS without carrying your data debt across.

The failure points of a migration are almost always the data, not the system. I audit and reconcile the data before you move it, while you move it, and after you have, and I build the native reporting suites and reports your teams run in the new HRIS.

What I do

  1. 01

    Data Readiness: Exposing Data Debt

    Organisations often commit to multi-million-pound HRIS contracts and then migrate years of legacy data into the new system, carrying its problems straight over. Before you move, I audit your legacy exports and EUCs to expose your exact data debt: the unvarnished evidence of what is broken, and a prioritised correction list so your admin teams can fix the records at source before the migration begins.

  2. 02

    HRIS Migration Data Assurance

    When HR data is moving from one system to another. From scoping through to extracting legacy data, checking hierarchies, and reconciling cutover loads. The failure points of a migration are almost always the data, not the system.

  3. 03

    Post-Migration Continuity & MSS

    When you move to a new HRIS, the business still needs answers on Day 1. I replicate and improve your native reporting, but crucially, I engineer Manager Self-Service (MSS) dashboards. This stops highly paid HR Business Partners from functioning as report-runners, giving them back the bandwidth to act strategically, while line managers get their figures instantly.

  4. 04

    Native HRIS Report Building

    When the answers should live where the data does. I build native reporting suites and individual reports inside your HRIS, so HR, managers and executives run them themselves, built to your agreed definitions and documented so your team can own them.

How a migration runs

  1. 1

    Scope

    Which modules and which systems, and which exports and spreadsheets hold the truth today.

  2. 2

    Audit

    Every legacy file read as it is, and a list of what is broken, missing or contradictory, ranked by what it would affect.

  3. 3

    Map

    How each field and each value in the old data lands in the new HRIS, agreed once and written down.

  4. 4

    Correct at source

    A prioritised list your admin teams fix in the old system, so the problem is not carried over.

  5. 5

    Reconcile

    Hierarchies checked and every cutover load reconciled to its source.

  6. 6

    Continue

    The reports the business needs on day one, replicated and improved.

These are the steps the Migration Assistant in the HR Intelligence engine works through, a model built from my own experience of HRIS migrations.
