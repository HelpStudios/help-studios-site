Privacy

What this site does with your information.

Short, because there is little to say. This site is a set of static pages.

  • No cookies, no tracking. The site sets no cookies, keeps nothing in your browser, and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. It loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies.
  • The contact form sends nothing. It opens a draft in your own email program, addressed to Help Studios. Nothing is sent until you press send in that program, and the site collects nothing itself.
  • If you email me, I receive what you choose to write and your email address, and I use them to reply. I do not sell or share them.
  • The file demonstration runs in your browser. Anything you try on the pages that read a file is processed on your own device and is not uploaded.
  • Hosting. The pages are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it receives the technical details of each page request (such as your network address) in its own logs, under its own terms.
  • Your rights. You can ask me what I hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing me from the contact section.
  • Who I am. Help Studios Ltd, Wirral, UK.
