Bespoke tool creation / enhancement

Tools built for the way your team works.

I am not a coder. I am an architect: I understand what the end user needs and how the pieces fit together, and I use modern AI-assisted development to build it, then stress test it before anyone relies on it.

What I do

  1. 01

    Bespoke tool creation / enhancement

    From making end-user computing models fit for purpose, to engineering bespoke data tools. You get a working tool your team can own, with its logic documented and tested.

The kind of tool

  1. A

    A recruitment tracker

    For a team that does not want a recruitment HRIS, just something it can work with: requisitions, candidates, sources and stages in one place, with source analysis.

  2. B

    An employee relations tracker

    Each adviser keeps their own tracking sheet. When they upload it, it joins one master view of grievances, disciplinaries, absence cases and data breaches, so nobody is locked out of a shared spreadsheet and the whole picture is in one place.

  3. C

    A spreadsheet model made fit for purpose

    Your existing end-user computing model, kept, with its logic documented, tested and protected.

How I build

  1. 1

    Specify

    With the people who will use it, in their words, before anything is built.

  2. 2

    Design

    The data and the screens, so the tool fits how the team works.

  3. 3

    Build

    With modern AI-assisted development, directed by an architect.

  4. 4

    Stress test

    Checks written to fail if something breaks, and tried on the messy files real teams send.

  5. 5

    Hand over

    The logic documented, so your team owns it.

Employee relations records are among the most sensitive in HR. Where the data lives, who can see which case and how long it is kept are decided at the start, not added at the end.
