Bespoke tool creation / enhancement

Fit-for-purpose tools, built by someone who knows what the end user needs.

From making end-user computing models fit for purpose, to engineering bespoke data tools. You get a working tool your team can own, with its logic documented and tested.

I am not a coder by background. My architectural experience means I understand what the end user needs and how to put it together, and I use modern AI-assisted development to build it properly, then stress test it.

Models I build

From a team’s own tracker to the MI the business reads.

Recruitment engine

Who uses it
Recruiters, hiring managers and the Head of Recruitment: a team that wants a tracker it can work with rather than a recruitment HRIS.
What it tracks
Job requisitions and openings, every application from applied to hired, sources, recruiters, offers and starts.
The MI it gives
Time to fill and to accept, drop-off by stage, offer acceptance, recruiter and source performance, early leavers by source, the cost of open vacancies, on one dashboard.

Learning dashboard

Who uses it
Each learner, their manager, and the learning and compliance team.
What it tracks
Mandatory modules with due and expiry dates, completions, training delivered and the educators who deliver it.
The MI it gives
Each learner’s own list of what is due, overdue and done; compliance by team and division; what expires next; educator KPIs.

Survey model

Who uses it
HR, People and Culture, and the owners of onboarding and exit.
What it tracks
Questionnaires built for you (new starter, exit, pulse), who is due from the headcount dates, and the send lists.
The MI it gives
Results by theme and statement, a recommend score with its base, a word picture that never keeps comments, and no group under ten shown.

Employee relations model

Who uses it
Each ER adviser, the ER lead and the HR Director.
What it tracks
Each adviser keeps their own tracker and uploads it into one central tracker of grievances, disciplinaries, absence cases and data breaches, so nobody is locked out of a shared spreadsheet.
The MI it gives
Caseload by adviser and type, days open and time to resolve, ACAS steps recorded, tribunal-risk prompts, FCA conduct flags and manager hot spots.
