HR and Finance alignment

HR and Finance month-end packs, reconciled.

Finance produces month-end packs and HR produces month-end packs. Some figures are fundamental and have to match: FTE, headcount, joiners and leavers.

Where they do not match, the reason is usually alignment. HR may align people by their manager hierarchies and Finance by cost centres. Finance may separate the people working on projects into one section, where HR prefers to keep them in their home division. There can be other reasons too, and I establish which apply in your organisation.

How I work

Both functions keep reporting the way they want. Every difference is explained.

  1. 01

    I establish the reasons

    I do not assume why the numbers differ. I find out how each function aligns and counts its people, and which differences that creates.

  2. 02

    I work within your requirements

    HR and Finance can each produce their report the way they need to. The aim is not to force one view on the other.

  3. 03

    I explain every difference

    Wherever a difference in the numbers is created, the delta is explained clearly, so the reconciliation can happen between two different numbers.
