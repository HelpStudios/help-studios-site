Chart library

The chart types the engine and its Power BI output use.

11 chart types, grouped by the job each does, each with a worked example drawn from the same synthetic Meridian Bank data. Pick the shape that answers the question, not the one that looks busiest.

Comparison 2Trend 1Part-to-whole 1Relationship 2Profile / radial 2Single number 1Detail 1Control 1

Comparison

Bar / column

Absence days per employee, by department

Question it answers: Which department, location or team has the most or least of something?

Compare one measure across categories side by side - the default for ranking teams on absence, attrition, headcount or pay position.

Combo (line + column)

Attrition rate against headcount

Question it answers: How does a rate move at the same time as the thing behind it?

Two measures on different scales in one frame, such as attrition rate against headcount, so a trend and its driver are read together.

Trend

Line

Absence rate over twelve months

Question it answers: Is this getting better or worse over time?

Show a measure across periods - absence rate, attrition, engagement - so the direction and any turning point stand out.

Part-to-whole

Doughnut / donut

Workforce by contract type

Question it answers: How is the workforce made up?

Show the proportions of a whole - contract type, gender or age band - when composition matters more than exact values.

Relationship

Scatter

Tenure versus flight risk

Question it answers: Are two things related, and are there outliers?

Plot one measure against another for each person or team - tenure against flight risk - to reveal correlation and outliers.

Bubble

Engagement versus absence, sized by team

Question it answers: Does a relationship hold once size is taken into account?

A scatter with a third dimension shown as bubble size - engagement against absence, sized by team headcount.

Profile / radial

Radar

Team profile versus the organisation

Question it answers: How does one team compare with the organisation across several factors at once?

Compare a team against the norm across five or six measures to show where it is strong or stretched.

Polar area

Sickness episodes by weekday

Question it answers: Is there a cyclical pattern, such as by weekday or month?

Show magnitude around a cycle - it picks out a Monday and Friday sickness pattern that an average would hide.

Single number

Card (KPI)

Headline KPIs with movement

Question it answers: What is the headline number right now?

One figure with its movement - headcount, FTE or absence rate - for the top of a board page.

Detail

Table / matrix

Department matrix of key measures

Question it answers: What are the exact figures behind the chart?

Exact values in a cross-tab - department by measure - for the reader who needs the number, not the shape.

Control

Slicer (filter)

Department filter

Question it answers: What happens when I look at just my department or site?

A filter control that re-cuts every chart on the page to a department, location or period, without rebuilding anything.
