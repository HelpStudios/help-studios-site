One department here starts 61.6% of its sickness episodes on a Monday or Friday; the others sit between 26.7% and 29.5%. A single organisation-wide figure would look ordinary. This view points straight at the days to look at.

The kind of signal the engine surfaces - and a person investigates.

Comparison

Bar / column

Contact Centre runs 2.5x the Finance absence rate.

Question it answers: Which department, location or team has the most or least of something?

Rank teams, locations or categories against each other on one measure - absence, attrition, headcount or pay.

Combo (line + column)

Attrition and headcount rise together over the year.

Question it answers: How does a rate move at the same time as the thing behind it?

Read a rate against the thing that drives it - attrition against headcount - on two scales in one frame.

Trend

Line

Absence has sat above the 5% target since June.

Question it answers: Is this getting better or worse over time?

Track a measure over time so the direction, and the point it turned, are obvious.

Part-to-whole

Doughnut / donut

A stable core: 78% of people are permanent.

Question it answers: How is the workforce made up?

Show the make-up of a whole - contract type, gender or age band - when composition matters more than exact figures.

Relationship

Scatter

Several long-tenure people still cross the flight-risk line.

Question it answers: Are two things related, and are there outliers?

Plot one measure against another for each person or team - tenure against flight risk - to show correlation and outliers.

Bubble

Operations is the largest team and the furthest from the norm.

Question it answers: Does a relationship hold once size is taken into account?

A scatter with a third dimension shown as size - engagement against absence, sized by team.

Profile / radial

Radar

Absence is the stretched axis; the rest track the organisation.

Question it answers: How does one team compare with the organisation across several factors at once?

Put one team against the norm across five or six measures - where it is strong, where it is stretched.

Polar area

Monday and Friday carry 61.6% of sickness episodes.

Question it answers: Is there a cyclical pattern, such as by weekday or month?

Show magnitude around a cycle - it catches a Monday and Friday sickness pattern that an average would hide.

Single number

Card (KPI)

Absence is 1.1 points above target - the number to watch.

Question it answers: What is the headline number right now?

One figure and its movement - headcount, FTE or absence rate - for the top of a board page.

Detail

Table / matrix

Contact Centre pairs a small team with the highest attrition.

Question it answers: What are the exact figures behind the chart?

The exact figures in a cross-tab - department by measure - for the reader who needs the number, not the shape.

Control

Slicer (filter)

Filtering to Retail lifts absence from 6.4% to 9.2%.

Question it answers: What happens when I look at just my department or site?

A filter that re-cuts every chart to a department, location or period, without rebuilding anything.
