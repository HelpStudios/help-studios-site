Headcount & FTE 4

Headcount
How many active employees there are, at the period end.
FTE Actual
Full-time-equivalent headcount: part-timers counted in proportion to their hours.
Division Headcount
Headcount of each REAL division (the mapped DIVISION field), read from the period's own stored condense snapshot divisionCut.
Division FTE
FTE of each real division, from the period's stored divisionCut.

Demographics & Population 7

AGE Band
Distribution of employees across age bands: Under 25, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55+.
Tenure Band
Distribution of employees across tenure bands: 0-1yr, 1-2yr, 2-5yr, 5-10yr, 10yr+.
Gender Split
Count of employees by declared gender (Female, Male, Non-binary)
Disability
Disability declaration rate: (employees declaring a disability) / (total employees who responded to the disability survey) × 100.
Ptft Ratio
Full time percentage: (full time employees) / (total employees with known contract type) × 100.
Working Pattern
Count of employees by working pattern type.
Retirement Risk
Count of employees within N years of retirement age.

Location & Geography 7

Site HC
Total count of employees mapped to a known site/location.
Site Gender
Distribution of employees by site location and gender.
AVG Commute
Average commute distance in miles for employees where both HOME_POSTCODE and WORK_POSTCODE are populated.
Commute Bands
Distribution of employees across commute distance bands.
Commute Risk
Commute risk delta: (average commute distance for leavers) - (average commute distance for stayers), in miles.
Work Arrangement
Count of employees by work arrangement type: Remote, Hybrid, Office-based.
Regional
Count of employees by geographic region.

Recruitment 11

Days TO Fill
Average calendar days from vacancy being approved/opened to candidate acceptance (offer accepted)
Time TO Hire
Average calendar days from requisition approval to candidate start date.
Time TO Offer
Average calendar days from job posting/publication to offer extended.
Offer Acceptance Rate
Percentage of offers accepted: (offers accepted) / (offers extended) × 100.
Applicants PER Vacancy
Average number of applicants per vacancy: total applicants / total vacancies open in the period.
Selection Ratio
Ratio of hires to applicants: (total hires) / (total applicants)
Internal VS External
Percentage of hires from internal sources: (internal hires) / (total hires) × 100.
Vacancy Rate
Vacancy rate: (open vacancies at period end) / (total established positions), expressed as percentage.
Pipeline REQ Posted
Pipeline conversion rate from requisition to posting: (requisitions that reached 'Posted' stage) / (total requisitions) × 100.
Median Days TO Fill
Median days from requisition to accepted offer, for filled roles.
Internal Hire PCT
Hires sourced internally as a share of all hires.

Training & Compliance 8

Training Compliance
Percentage of mandatory training modules completed: (completed mandatory modules) / (total mandatory modules assigned), multiplied by 100.
Regulatory Breach
Regulatory breach risk level mapped to numeric scale: Low=0, Medium=1, High=2, Critical=3.
Training Overdue
Count of training modules past their due date with status 'Not Started' or 'In Progress'.
Training AVG Days
Average calendar days to complete training modules: average of (COMPLETION_DATE - ASSIGNED_DATE) across all completed modules in the period.
Compliance Cost
Compliance cost exposure: weighted total of overdue mandatory modules multiplied by a cost-per-module estimate.
Training Delivered
Count of learning completions inside the period that are NOT mandatory compliance training.
Training Delivered Learners
Distinct learners behind Training Delivered: employees with at least one non-mandatory completion in the period month.
Division Training Compliance
Mandatory training compliance rate (percent) of each real division.

Performance & Talent 4

Rating Dist
Total count of employees with a recorded performance rating.
Rating PAY
Average pay increase percentage for employees rated 'Exceeds Expectations'.
HP Turnover
High performer turnover rate: (leavers rated Exceeds Expectations) / (total employees rated Exceeds Expectations in prior period) × 100.
High Performer PCT
Ratings classified Exceeds or Exceptional.

Pay 3

Median Salary
Median annual base salary across active people.
PAY Changes People
People whose salary changed between the previous month's compensation file and this month's.
PAY Changes Annual Value
Annual value of those salary changes, rises less decreases, rounded to whole pounds (the 'Annual impact' tile of the Compare pay-changes lens, month against previous month)

Engagement & Wellbeing 10

ENG Survey Response
Engagement survey response rate: (employees who completed the survey) / (employees invited to participate) × 100.
Enps Score
Employee Net Promoter Score: % Promoters (score 9-10) - % Detractors (score 0-6)
ENG Score AVG
Average engagement score across all surveyed employees.
Wellbeing AVG
Average wellbeing composite score across all surveyed employees.
MGR Quality
Manager quality index: average score on manager effectiveness questions in the engagement survey.
ENG Dept
Count of departments with engagement survey data.
ENG Tenure
Count of tenure bands with engagement data.
Wellbeing Dept
Count of departments with wellbeing data.
Would Recommend
Would recommend rate: percentage of employees who would recommend the organisation as a great place to work.
Intent TO Leave
Intent to leave rate: percentage of employees indicating they are actively looking to leave or likely to leave within 12 months.

Diversity & Inclusion 8

Wifc Rate
Women in Finance Charter percentage: percentage of women in senior management and executive roles.
Wifc Dept
Count of departments with WiFC data available.
Women IN Leadership
Percentage of leadership roles held by women.
Gender Seniority
Total count of employees classified by gender and seniority level.
Ethnicity Seniority
Total count of employees classified by ethnicity and seniority level.
Promotion Rate Gender
Promotion rate by gender: (employees promoted) / (total employees in that gender group) × 100.
Promotion Rate Ethnicity
Promotion rate by ethnicity: (employees promoted) / (total employees in each ethnic group) × 100.
Disability Seniority
Total count of employees classified by disability declaration and seniority level.

Employee Relations 13

ER Case Volume
Total count of employee relations cases opened in the period.
ER Case Rate
Case rate per 1,000 employees: (total case volume / headcount) × 1,000.
ER Resolution Time
Average calendar days from case opening to case closure.
ER Substantiation
Substantiation rate: (cases where grievance/complaint was upheld or partially upheld) / (total closed cases) × 100.
ER Disciplinary Dept
Count of departments with disciplinary case data.
ER Category
Total cases by category: sum of all case counts across category groupings (grievance, disciplinary, bullying, discrimination, whistleblowing, etc.)
ER Tribunal
Count of employment tribunal or litigation cases in the period.
ER Settlement Cost
Total settlement cost in reporting currency: sum of all settlement amounts for cases resolved in the period.
ER Repeat Offender
Repeat offender rate: (employees with 2+ cases in the trailing 12 months) / (total employees with at least 1 case) × 100.
ER Open Ageing
Count of open cases older than 90 days.
ER Open Cases
Cases with status Open at the period end.
Tribunal Risk High PCT
Cases assessed high or critical tribunal risk.
Settlement Spend
Settlement amounts plus external legal costs.

Attrition & Retention 21

Turnover
Turnover rate (in-month): total leavers classified by type (voluntary, involuntary, end-of-contract, retirement) divided by headcount, multiplied by 100.
Attrition R12
The share of people who left over a rolling 12 months, as a percentage.
Early Attrition
Early attrition rate for leavers within X days of start: (leavers with tenure <= threshold) / (total leavers) × 100.
Attrition Inmonth
In-month attrition rate: leavers in the summary month divided by the headcount denominator in the client's chosen method (attrition.denominatorMethod: start, end or average of...
Retention
Retention rate: 100% - turnoverRate.
Regrettable
Regrettable turnover rate: leavers with performance rating at or above the client high-performance threshold / total leavers × 100.
Stability
Stability index: percentage of employees who have been with the organisation for 12+ months.
Turnover MGR
Count of distinct managers found in the leaver-by-manager analysis.
Turnover Tenure
Total leavers classified by tenure band.
Turnover Reason
Total leavers by reason category.
Turnover Cost
Estimated annual turnover cost: total leavers × average salary × replacementCostMultiplier.
Flight Risk
Count of employees flagged as high flight risk.
Division Leavers
Counted leavers in the month by real division (stored divisionCut.leavers, from the attrition authority's counted leavers with exclusions applied)
Leaver Impact Planned Manager Exits
People with a leave date after the month end who lead at least one person.
Leaver Impact Orphaned Managers
Managers who are not on the month's active headcount but still appear as the manager of someone who is.
Leaver Impact Orphaned Reports
People on the month's active headcount whose manager is no longer on it.
Retention Risk RED
Count of employees categorised as high retention risk (red)
Voluntary Leavers
Leavers classified voluntary by the value taxonomy.
Involuntary Leavers
Dismissal, redundancy, ill-health, death, mutual agreement.
Attrition PCT
Leavers divided by average headcount.
Voluntary Attrition PCT
Voluntary leavers divided by average headcount.

Absence & Sickness 29

Absence Rate
Days lost to sickness as a percentage of days available, annualised over 12 months.
Absence R12
Rolling 12-month absence rate: sickness working days lost over the trailing 12 loaded months ÷ available working days × 100.
Days Lost EMP
Average working days lost per employee: total working days lost in period / headcount.
Frequency Rate
Absence frequency rate: number of absence spells per employee.
Absence Reason
Total working days lost categorised by absence reason.
Clustering
Monday clustering percentage: percentage of absence episodes that start on a Monday.
Long Short Term
Split of absence days into long-term (continuous absence > longTermThresholdDays, default 28) vs short-term.
Absence Cost
Estimated annual cost of absence: total working days lost × (average daily salary)
RTW Compliance
Return-to-work interview compliance percentage: (absence episodes with RTW completed) / (total absence episodes) × 100.
Bradford
Average Bradford Factor score across employee population.
Absence Trend
Latest month's days lost value from the trend time series.
Absence Inmonth
In-month absence rate: days lost in the summary month divided by the working days available in that same month, x 100.
Division Absence Rate
In-month absence rate (percent) of each real division: the in-month ledger's days summed by the employee's division over the division's headcount and the same available days per...
Sickness Days
Working days lost to sickness only; holiday, parental, carer and unauthorised excluded.
Absence Days ALL
All working days lost, including non-sickness absence.
Sickness Rate PCT
Sickness days lost as a percentage of available working days.
Absence Spells
Number of absence spells, not days.
Absence Other Share PCT
Share of absence rows whose reason resolved to OTHER — readable but uncategorised.
Absence Unmapped Share PCT
Share of absence rows whose reason column is blank or unreadable.
Leave Entitlement Days
Total annual leave entitlement across the population.
Leave Taken Days
Leave taken in the leave year to date.
Leave Balance Days
Remaining leave days across the population.
Leave Utilisation PCT
Leave taken as a share of entitlement.
Negative Balance Rows
Rows where the balance is below zero.
Leave Requests
Leave requests raised in the period.
Leave Approval PCT
Requests approved or authorised as a share of all requests.
Pending Requests
Requests still awaiting a decision.
AVG Days TO Start
Mean notice between the period end and the first day requested.
Leave Employees With Entitlement
Employees with a valid annual-leave entitlement in the month's LEAVE_BALANCE file: the 'Employees with entitlement' tile on the Leave Balance screen.

Movement 2

Joiners
People whose START_DATE falls inside the period.
Leavers
People whose LEAVE_DATE falls inside the period.

Reconciliation 1

Headcount Reconciled
Opening headcount + joiners - leavers equals closing headcount.

Data Quality 1

DQ Score
Data quality score: (informational findings) / (total findings) × 100.

Definitions are generated from the engine metric catalogue. Every figure is calculated from the files you load and the thresholds you set, never from baked-in benchmarks.
