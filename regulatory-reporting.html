Regulatory reporting

Regulatory reporting, built on governed data.

In regulated financial services, HR owes accurate, timely reporting to the board and the regulator. I build that reporting, and the governed, GDPR-compliant data underneath it.

On training compliance, I shortened the reporting turnaround to close to real time. People who had completed their training showed as complete straight away, so nobody could say the report was days out of date, and it kept its credibility.

What I report on

The reporting a regulated firm owes.

  1. Mandatory training compliance

    Who is due, overdue and complete, by team and division, turned round fast enough that the people on it trust it.

  2. Women in Finance Charter

    Women in senior roles against the firm’s Charter target, with the base each figure is drawn from.

  3. Gender pay gap and statutory returns

    The returns prepared and checked back to source.

  4. SM&CR conduct training

    Conduct training currency for people in certified and regulated roles.

  5. Block leave for regulated roles

    Whether people in roles that require it have taken their continuous block of leave.

  6. Governed, GDPR-compliant data

    Data models and internal analytics standards that keep personal data protected and every figure traceable.
